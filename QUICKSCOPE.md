GRZ Quickscope v0.1.1 — custom test build

This is a custom quickscope approximation, not an exact 1.29 rollback. The official 1.29 (86974a0, build 1.29.163709) and 1.30 (78be1c4, build 1.30.164289) script snapshots have the same scope-entry transition constants and weapon-raise completion logic. Relevant scoped-camera changes concern night vision; native engine, weapon config and animation changes cannot be reconstructed from this script diff. No 1.29 engine or animation files are bundled.

Changes:

- Normal weapon camera transitions into and out of iron sights/optics are capped at 0.08 seconds. Handheld binoculars, vehicle, unconscious, jump and climb camera transitions retain native timing.
- The extra script weapon-raise delay completes as soon as a living, conscious player is natively raised and requesting ADS. Native obstruction, sprint, inventory/action and weapon-manager checks still run. This does not force firing while sprinting or reloading, or speed up bolt cycling or reload animations.
- Additional camera reticle lag and movement bob are removed while using weapon sight cameras. The separate no-sway addon handles breathing/wandering. ADS/quickscope mouse, hand and camera-Z recoil modifiers are reduced to 10% of their native values, sampled once per shot so unscoping cannot restore full kick mid-shot. Hipfire recoil and injury effects retain their native values. Custom recoil implementations that bypass RecoilBase.GetRecoilModifier may need their own integration.
- Supported GRZ Hitscan shots now align toward an aim-ray contact for both hipfire and ADS. The server uses its per-player camera transform when available and near the player, facing the replicated aim; otherwise it falls back to the head position and replicated base aiming angles plus hand offsets. A camera/head ray finds the aim point, then the damage ray runs from the actual muzzle toward that point. Nearby muzzle cover can still block it. Free-look/stale/spectator camera directions are not used to redirect shots. No client-supplied target or damage RPC is introduced.

Requires the matching updated GRZ_Hitscan.pbo and GRZ_NoSway.pbo. All five recent updates are in GRZ-latest-mod-updates.zip: Quickscope, corrected Hitscan, NoSway, AntiHardscope and Killfeed. Merge @GRZ_Local into BOTH game and server installations; preserve custom weapons and your generated inventory PBO. Restart both. The 0.75-second anti-hardscope timer continues to start on actual optics entry and is not changed by quickscope.

The native camera-shooting flag is also enabled in weapon sight views and regular weapon cameras, covering native guns outside the scripted hitscan set. Native dispersion, shotgun spread and projectile ballistics remain on unsupported ammunition. This does not force every pellet onto one point.

Limitations: server hitscan alignment applies only to the supported single-muzzle, nonexplosive, single-projectile GRZ Hitscan firearms and filtered target families described in GRZ-Hitscan-TEST.md. It cannot promise every custom gun's native animation, sight axis or networked aiming angles match its displayed reticle. Without a rendered server camera, the head/aim fallback cannot guarantee pixel-perfect third-person parallax at every distance. Native camera flag behavior and custom guns need runtime verification. Unsupported pellet/multi-muzzle/explosive weapons retain native projectile damage, spread and flight. Scope timing is a camera blend cap, not a replacement of all native raising animations.

Required in-game checks: first/third person, standing/crouched/prone ADS; tap shots before and after full scope entry at several distances; native and custom UAV guns; armour and head/body hits; nearby walls/cover; sprint, reload, bolt cycling, unconsciousness and death; hipfire; single and repeated quickscope shots and immediate unscope with 10% recoil; handheld binoculars; 0.75-second blackout and reset; no delayed duplicate hits. Check that server-replicated aim follows client aim under normal latency. These behaviors are unverified in this workspace because no DayZ/Enforce runtime is available.

To disable only quickscope, remove GRZ_Quickscope.pbo from BOTH sides and restart. Corrected GRZ_Hitscan.pbo defaults to its original muzzle ray without this addon. No save or mission files are changed.
