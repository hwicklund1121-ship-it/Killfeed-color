Match its transparent black backing, black centered heading, white labels and native arrow selectors. Keep clouds. Preview the actual sky as colors change, Apply saves locally and Cancel restores the previous selection. Include Default, Black, Red, Blue, Pink, Purple, Cyan, Green, Orange and Yellow.

Their format is still needed before the renderer can be implemented while preserving clouds. Use the downloadable GRZ-sky-lighting-extractor.zip to locate and copy the two resources from installed PBOs without guessing archive names. No whole-screen color filter or cloud-occluding dome is used.
